Business Case

From insurance data to decision-ready insight

The project simulates a Business Intelligence and Analytics requirement inside a multinational life insurance environment: monitor a competitive market, validate key indicators and transform analytical results into information that can support management decisions.

CENTRAL QUESTION

How is the simulated insurance market evolving, and which signals reveal meaningful changes in growth, market share, technical performance, product mix and portfolio development?

Executive Recommendations

From analytical signals to possible business actions

Recommendations are framed as analytical scenarios rather than prescriptions for real insurance companies.

01

Evaluate growth quality

Monitor premium expansion together with Claims Ratio, Expense Ratio and Technical Margin instead of evaluating volume alone.

02

Monitor competition

Track quarterly market-share movements alongside premium growth to distinguish market expansion from competitive gains.

03

Preserve technical discipline

Monitor claims, expenses and technical margin as the portfolio expands to identify potential deterioration early.

04

Investigate portfolio decline

Extend future analysis with policy-level data to study renewals, cancellations, acquisition cohorts and retention behavior.

Methodological Note

All companies, observations and analytical results used in this project are 100% synthetic and were created exclusively for educational and portfolio purposes.

The Composite Solvency Ratio is a project-specific analytical indicator and does not reproduce the official regulatory solvency methodology of Chile's Comisión para el Mercado Financiero (CMF).

Net Portfolio Change should not automatically be interpreted as churn, retention or renewal, and customer-level behavioral conclusions would require additional analytical granularity.
